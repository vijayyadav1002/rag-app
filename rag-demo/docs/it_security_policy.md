# IT Security Policy

## Password Requirements
- Passwords must be at least 14 characters and include a mix of upper/lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols.
- Passwords must be rotated every 180 days for systems that store customer PII.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for all company systems, including email, VPN, and the HR portal.

## Device Security
- Company laptops must have disk encryption (FileVault/BitLocker) enabled at all times.
- Screens must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.
- Personal devices used to access company email must be enrolled in the Mobile Device Management (MDM) system.

## Data Handling
- Customer PII may not be stored on local drives, USB drives, or personal cloud storage (e.g., personal Google Drive, Dropbox).
- Customer data must only be accessed through approved systems (Salesforce, the internal Support Console) and only for legitimate business purposes.

## Incident Reporting
Suspected security incidents (phishing emails, lost devices, suspected breaches) must be reported to security@northwindretail.example within 1 hour of discovery via the Security Incident Form.
