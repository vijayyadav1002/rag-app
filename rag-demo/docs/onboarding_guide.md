# New Employee Onboarding Guide

## Before Day 1
- IT ships your laptop and equipment (see Remote Work Equipment Policy for what's included) so it arrives by your start date.
- HR sends you an offer packet with benefits enrollment instructions; benefits elections are due within 30 days of hire.

## Day 1
- Attend the New Hire Orientation (virtual, 9am ET) covering company overview, code of conduct, and IT setup.
- Set up MFA on your accounts per the IT Security Policy.
- Meet with your manager to review your 30/60/90 day plan.

## Week 1
- Complete mandatory compliance training: Data Privacy, Workplace Conduct, and Security Awareness, all due within 7 days.
- Get access to team tools (Slack, Jira, Confluence) — requested automatically based on your role in Workday.

## First 30 Days
- Enroll in benefits (health, dental, vision, 401k) — see the Benefits Enrollment Guide for details.
- Schedule 1:1s with key cross-functional partners identified by your manager.

## Questions
Onboarding questions go to the People Team via the #onboarding-help Slack channel or hr@northwindretail.example.
