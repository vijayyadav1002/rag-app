# Sick Leave Policy

## Overview
Sick Leave is separate from PTO and is intended for an employee's own illness, injury, or medical appointments, or to care for an immediate family member who is ill.

## Accrual
- All employees, including part-time, accrue 1 hour of Sick Leave for every 30 hours worked, up to a maximum of 48 hours (6 days) per calendar year.
- Sick Leave accrues starting on an employee's first day of employment.

## Usage
- Sick Leave may be used in increments as small as 1 hour.
- No advance notice is required for unplanned illness, but employees should notify their manager before their shift begins when possible.
- For absences of 3 or more consecutive days, a doctor's note may be requested by HR.

## Carryover
Unused Sick Leave carries over year to year, but the total accrued balance may never exceed 96 hours (12 days). Sick Leave is not paid out upon termination.

## Relationship to PTO
Employees should use Sick Leave, not PTO, for illness-related absences. HR may recharacterize a PTO request as Sick Leave if the stated reason is medical.
