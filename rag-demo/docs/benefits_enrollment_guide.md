# Benefits Enrollment Guide

## Enrollment Window
New employees must enroll in benefits within 30 days of their start date via the Workday Benefits portal. Missing this window means waiting until the next Open Enrollment period (November) unless you have a qualifying life event.

## Health Insurance
- Three medical plan tiers are offered: Bronze, Silver, and Gold, all administered by BlueShield Northwind.
- Northwind Retail Co. covers 80% of the employee premium and 60% of dependent premiums for the Silver plan.
- Dental and vision are separate opt-in plans, fully employee-paid via payroll deduction.

## 401(k)
- Employees are auto-enrolled at a 3% contribution rate after 60 days unless they opt out.
- Northwind matches 50% of contributions up to 6% of salary, vesting over 4 years (25% per year).

## Qualifying Life Events
Marriage, divorce, birth/adoption of a child, or loss of other coverage allow benefits changes outside Open Enrollment. Changes must be submitted within 30 days of the event.
