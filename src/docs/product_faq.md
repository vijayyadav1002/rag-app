# Product FAQ — Northwind Home Smart Thermostat

## Setup
Q: How do I connect the thermostat to Wi-Fi?
A: Open the Northwind Home app, tap "Add Device," and follow the on-screen prompts. The thermostat must be on a 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network — it does not support 5GHz networks.

Q: The app can't find my thermostat during setup. What should I do?
A: Ensure Bluetooth is enabled on your phone (used only for initial pairing), restart the thermostat by holding the front button for 10 seconds, and make sure you're within 15 feet of the device during setup.

## Usage
Q: How do I set a schedule?
A: In the app, go to Schedule > Add Period, choose a temperature and time range for Weekdays or Weekends. Schedules sync to the device within 30 seconds of saving.

Q: Does the thermostat learn my habits automatically?
A: Yes, if "Smart Learning" is enabled in Settings, the thermostat adjusts its schedule after 7 days of manual overrides at consistent times.

## Troubleshooting
Q: My thermostat shows "E3" on the display. What does that mean?
A: E3 indicates a wiring fault, typically a loose C-wire (common wire). Power off at the breaker, verify wiring against the included diagram, and power back on.

Q: The thermostat is offline in the app.
A: Check that your Wi-Fi router hasn't changed passwords or networks. Hold the front button for 5 seconds to see the connection status on the device screen. A factory reset (hold button 20 seconds) is a last resort.

## Warranty
The thermostat carries a 2-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects, not damage from incorrect wiring installation.
