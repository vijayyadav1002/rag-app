# Remote Work Equipment Policy

## Overview
This policy covers equipment provided to remote and hybrid employees. It does not cover travel expenses, which are addressed in the Travel & Expense Reimbursement Policy.

## Standard Equipment
New remote employees receive a laptop, monitor, keyboard, mouse, and headset within their first week, shipped by IT.

## Home Office Stipend
Remote employees receive a one-time $500 home office setup stipend, submitted as a reimbursement within 60 days of start date, and an ongoing $40/month internet stipend added to payroll.

## Equipment Requests
Additional equipment requests (second monitor, ergonomic chair, etc.) go through the IT Service Portal and require manager approval for items over $200.

## Return of Equipment
All company-owned equipment must be returned within 10 business days of separation from the company. Unreturned equipment may be deducted from final pay where permitted by state law.
