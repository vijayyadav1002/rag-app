# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

## Overview
Full-time employees at Northwind Retail Co. accrue Paid Time Off (PTO) that can be used for vacation, personal days, or minor illness. PTO is separate from the Sick Leave Policy and the Parental Leave Policy.

## Accrual
- Employees accrue 1.25 days of PTO per month, totaling 15 days per year.
- Employees with 5+ years of tenure accrue 1.67 days per month, totaling 20 days per year.
- PTO accrual begins on an employee's start date and accrues on the last day of each calendar month.

## Carryover
Employees may carry over up to 5 unused PTO days into the next calendar year. Any PTO balance beyond 5 days that is not used by December 31st is forfeited, except where prohibited by state law.

## Requesting PTO
PTO requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 business days in advance for requests longer than 2 days. Manager approval is required. Same-day PTO requests should be communicated directly to your manager as early as possible.

## Payout on Termination
Unused, accrued PTO is paid out at the employee's final base rate upon separation from the company, in accordance with state law.
