# Parental Leave Policy

## Overview
Northwind Retail Co. provides paid parental leave for employees welcoming a new child through birth, adoption, or foster placement. This is separate from Sick Leave and PTO.

## Eligibility
Employees are eligible after 90 days of continuous full-time employment.

## Leave Duration
- Birthing parent: 12 weeks paid leave, which may run concurrently with short-term disability leave.
- Non-birthing parent or adoptive/foster parent: 6 weeks paid leave.
- Leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth, adoption, or placement date.

## Pay During Leave
Parental leave is paid at 100% of base salary. It does not draw down PTO or Sick Leave balances.

## Requesting Leave
Employees should notify HR and their manager at least 30 days before the anticipated leave start date when possible. A Parental Leave Request Form must be submitted through the HR portal.

## Returning to Work
Employees are guaranteed reinstatement to the same or an equivalent position upon return, consistent with applicable federal and state law.
