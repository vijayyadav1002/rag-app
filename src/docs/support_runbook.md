# Customer Support Runbook — Northwind Home Smart Thermostat

## Tier 1: First Response
- Verify the customer's device model and firmware version (Settings > About in the app).
- Check the Known Issues board before troubleshooting — many "offline" reports during firmware rollout weeks are already tracked.
- For wiring/E-code errors, refer to the Product FAQ troubleshooting section and confirm the customer has power at the breaker before proceeding.

## Escalation Criteria
Escalate to Tier 2 if:
- The issue persists after a factory reset and reinstallation.
- The customer reports a burning smell, sparking, or visible damage — instruct the customer to disconnect power immediately and escalate as a Safety Incident, not a standard ticket.
- The device is bricked (does not power on at all) and is within the 2-year warranty window — route to RMA (Return Merchandise Authorization).

## Refunds & Replacements
- Devices within 30 days of purchase: full refund or replacement, no questions asked, processed through the Support Console.
- Devices within the 2-year warranty but past 30 days: replacement only, requires proof of purchase and confirmation the defect is not from customer wiring error.
- Devices out of warranty: offer a 20% loyalty discount code for a new unit; no free replacement.

## Response Time SLAs
- Chat: respond within 2 minutes.
- Email: respond within 4 business hours.
- Safety Incidents: escalate immediately, response within 15 minutes, loop in the Safety team via #safety-escalations.
