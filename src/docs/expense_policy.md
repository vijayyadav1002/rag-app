# Travel & Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Overview
This policy covers reimbursement for business-related travel, meals, and incidental expenses. It does not cover home office equipment, which is covered by the Remote Work Equipment Policy.

## Booking Travel
- Flights and hotels must be booked through the corporate travel portal (Concur) whenever possible.
- Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is permitted for flights over 6 hours with manager pre-approval.

## Meal Reimbursement
- Daily meal per diem while traveling: $75/day domestic, $100/day international.
- Alcohol is not reimbursable except at client-facing dinners, up to $40 per event, with a valid business justification noted.

## Submitting Expenses
- All expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date via the Expensify system.
- Receipts are required for any single expense over $25.
- Expense reports are approved by the employee's direct manager and reimbursed within 2 pay cycles.

## Non-Reimbursable Items
Personal entertainment, traffic/parking fines, and airline club memberships are not reimbursable.
