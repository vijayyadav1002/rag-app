# Code of Conduct

## Core Principles
Northwind Retail Co. employees are expected to act with integrity, respect, and professionalism in all interactions with colleagues, customers, and partners.

## Conflicts of Interest
Employees must disclose any financial interest in a vendor, competitor, or customer to their manager and Legal before engaging with that party in a business capacity. Undisclosed conflicts of interest may result in termination.

## Anti-Harassment
Northwind has zero tolerance for harassment or discrimination based on race, gender, age, religion, disability, sexual orientation, or any other protected class. Reports can be made confidentially to HR or via the anonymous Ethics Hotline.

## Gifts & Entertainment
Employees may not accept gifts from vendors or customers valued over $75. Business meals with clients are permitted but must be reported if they exceed $200 per person.

## Reporting Violations
Violations of this Code of Conduct should be reported to HR, your manager, or the anonymous Ethics Hotline at ethics@northwindretail.example. Retaliation against good-faith reporters is strictly prohibited.
